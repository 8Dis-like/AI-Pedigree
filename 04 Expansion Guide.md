# Expansion guide

1. Search the name register before creating a person or organization, including Chinese aliases and alternative transliterations.
2. Establish identity with an official university, employer, professional society, thesis repository or first-person profile.
3. Record a relationship as one claim: source entity, precise relation, target entity, dates, evidence URL or PDF page, and status V/B/D.
4. Use degree records or explicit supervisor lists for academic genealogy. Shared affiliations, papers and friendship do not prove supervision.
5. Keep historical roles and organizational reorganizations separate. DeepMind, Google Brain and the combined Google DeepMind have separate nodes.
6. Update the entity notes and evidence JSON when changing a factual claim; then draw or edit the canvas edge. Preserve original aliases and stable IDs.
7. Retain disagreements in the review queue. Do not silently replace a historical claim with an unchecked current title.
