# FinEvo: Recursive Self-Improvement for Long-Horizon Financial Modeling

**Source:** [ArXiv – cs.AI](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.28239)
**Imported:** 2026-11-24 15:22 UTC

## Abstract / Summary

arXiv:2611.28239v1 Announce Type: new Abstract: Advances in large language models (LLMs) have expanded their role in high-value productivity tasks, including financial analysis and modeling, while enabling models to play an increasingly active role in their own improvement. This motivates two complementary lines of inquiry: can financial modeling capabilities provide a foundation for model self-evolution, and can self-evolution, in turn, advance financial capabilities? We introduce FinEvo, a self-evolution framework that integrates autonomous experience construction with financial capability improvement. FinEvo prepares financial models, iteratively evolves challenges through self-play, and learns from the resulting interactions using executable rewards, relying on the same model throughout. An end-to-end execution with Qwen3.6-27B shows that solving feedback supports both increasing reasoning demands and identifying specification issues that executable checks can miss. Learning from these experiences raises whole-task success from 22.73 to 29.09 on held-out within-source tasks and from 13.13 to 19.19 on SpreadsheetBench 2 Financial. Comparable gains across task sources and evaluation settings, together with improvement on a task category absent from training, suggest that FinEvo strengthens underlying financial modeling capabilities shared across tasks. These findings support self-evolution as a path toward broader financial competence, advancing the empirical exploration of recursive self-improvement (RSI) in professional domains. Code is available at https://github.com/pcj-sudo/FinEvo.
