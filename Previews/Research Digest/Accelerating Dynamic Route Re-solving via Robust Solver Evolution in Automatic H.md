# Accelerating Dynamic Route Re-solving via Robust Solver Evolution in Automatic Heuristic Design

**Source:** [ArXiv – cs.NE (Neural & Evolutionary Computing)](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.32576)
**Imported:** 2026-11-25 06:00 UTC

## Abstract / Summary

arXiv:2611.32576v1 Announce Type: new Abstract: Multi-goal route planning uses spatial data on service locations and pairwise travel costs to determine a visiting order that minimizes total travel cost. This task can be formulated as the traveling salesman problem (TSP) and has benefited from large language model-based automatic heuristic design (AHD) for constructing effective solvers. Despite their effectiveness in static settings, existing methods exhibit substantial re-solving inefficiency when location changes, new requests, or request cancellations alter the underlying request data during execution. Our empirical analysis suggests that ``static-overfitting'' contributes to this inefficiency, as evolved solvers may repeatedly direct search toward overlapping local neighborhoods after dynamic changes. We further find that this ``overfitting'' is associated with limitations in existing evolutionary processes, where lower objective values on some instances coincide with longer solving times. In this paper, we therefore propose ERDR, a plug-and-play framework that integrates two strategies into existing AHD pipelines to guide evolution toward solvers that re-solve dynamically changed instances faster. Specifically, 1) ERDR uses validation-guided reselection to reevaluate candidates on instances excluded from routine evolution, reducing specialization to fixed training instances. 2) ERDR applies runtime robustness-augmented evaluation, supplementing the original evaluation objective of AHD methods with inter-group variability and tail risk in solving time to discourage costly and uneven search. Experiments across multiple TSP scales and dynamic scenarios show that ERDR achieves average re-solving speedups of $2.2\times$ under guided local search and $4.4\times$ under iterated local search.
