# Stable Gated DeltaNet-2: Understanding and Mitigating Recurrent State Instability

## Abstract / Summary

Authors: Xichong Zhang, Xixuan Hao, Zhichen Liu, Le Zhou, Zeyu Zhang, Boyu Chen, Mingfei Lu, Zehao Lin, Bo Tang, Yonggang Zhang, Linyun Liu, Feiyu Xiong, Jianlin Su, Zhiyu Li Upvotes: 60 Summary: Linear attention offers an efficient solution for long-sequence modeling via linear complexity and fixed-size recurrent states. Gated DeltaNet-2 (GDN-2) introduces independent channel-wise control over erasure and writing, enhancing state-update expressiveness and in-context retrieval, yet it suffers from training failures during long-context training. We trace these failures to the asymmetry between reading and modification directions during erasure, which yields non-normal erase operators whose directional gains accumulate across tokens, triggering exponential state growth even without new writes. To resolve this, we propose , employing symmetric erasure to ensure non-expansive state propagation and decay-regulated residual writing to preserve writing flexibility. Theoretically, we establish an O(T) upper bound on recurrent state growth while retaining chunkwise parallel computation. Experiments on ~1.6B parameter models show that achieves stable training alongside improved long-context retrieval. Compared to GDN-2, it boosts the 128K average RULER score by 6.80 points in the recurrent setting and average accuracy across nine downstream tasks by 0.62 points in the hybrid setting.
