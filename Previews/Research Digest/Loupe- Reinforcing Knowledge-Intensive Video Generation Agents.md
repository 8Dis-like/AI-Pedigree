# Loupe: Reinforcing Knowledge-Intensive Video Generation Agents

**Source:** [HuggingFace – Daily Papers](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.31895)
**Imported:** 2026-11-26 05:54 UTC

## Abstract / Summary

Authors: Kai Zeng, Ruichuan An, Zhen Fang, Xi Fang, Han Lyu, Moran Li, Yingchen Yu, Yifan Dai, Bohan Zeng, Quanqing Xu, Zhengzhou Zhu, Wentao Zhang Upvotes: 1 Summary: Knowledge-intensive video generation remains challenging because prompts often omit the facts, appearances, and temporal relationships required for accurate generation. While search-based workflows can retrieve missing evidence, manually designed strategies limit adaptive planning, and conventional training suffers from costly video-level feedback and coarse trajectory-level credit assignment. In this paper, we present Loupe, a video generation agent that retrieves and verifies textual and visual evidence to construct evidence-grounded temporal video prompts with reference images. We further introduce Rubric-Based Credit (RBC), which leverages prompt-specific factual, visual, and temporal requirements to evaluate intermediate search and planning decisions before video rendering. Building on RBC, RBC-SFT reweights assistant-token learning by step quality, while RBC-GRPO incorporates step-level credits alongside a final planning reward, without rendering videos during RL. To address the lack of dedicated search-oriented evaluation, we construct Search-VBench, which contains 150 held-out prompts across seven categories. Experiments show that Loupe improves all four evaluation dimensions across three video generators, raising the overall score of MiniMax-H3 from 32.80 to 70.67 and outperforming an untrained search workflow. Ablation experiments show that full RBC training outperforms standard SFT followed by trajectory-level GRPO by 5.27 points. Human evaluations and results on Sci-VBench and KIVI-Bench further demonstrate improved generation quality, scientific grounding, factual precision, and helpfulness. Our project page is available at https://zengkaiya.github.io/Loupe/.
