# S-Mind: Internalizing Multi-View Geometry for Spatial Intelligence

**Source:** [HuggingFace – Daily Papers](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.28649)
**Imported:** 2026-11-25 06:00 UTC

## Abstract / Summary

Authors: Muxin Fu, Peike Yu, Guibin Zhang, Wenrui Zhou, Runze Liu, Lang Feng, Fuchao Yang, Feng Chen, Yixuan Li, Mengao Zhang, Shiying Zhang, Wen Shen, Ling Pan, Shuicheng Yan, Bo An Upvotes: 23 Summary: Spatial intelligence, the ability to reason about 3D space from visual observations, becomes essential as multimodal large language models (MLLMs) move into the physical world. Beyond perceiving each view, it rests on two cornerstones of human cognition: fusing multiple views into one coherent 3D scene and mentally transforming that scene to new viewpoints as reasoning unfolds. Yet our spatial probes reveal that MLLMs largely lack both capabilities, even after spatial fine-tuning. We therefore present S-Mind, which internalizes multi-view geometry within the MLLM through a compact set of spatial tokens. It supervises these tokens to hold the fused 3D scene and re-emits them to re-express this scene from every viewpoint the reasoning adopts. Across four spatial benchmarks, S-Mind improves its Qwen3.5 backbones by up to 22.26 points and surpasses frontier general models such as GPT-6-Astra (+0.45), Kimi-K3.1-Pro (+1.97), and GLM-5.3-Flash (+7.80) in average accuracy. Moreover, the same probes confirm that it acquires both capabilities, reaching fusion and transformation accuracies of 65.11% and 82.17%, against at most 16.02% and 37.28% for the baselines. At inference, S-Mind also runs about 100x faster than a tool-based agent on the same backbone. Instilled through these spatial tokens during training, spatial capability becomes intrinsic to the backbone and persists even when the tokens are withheld, offering a promising path toward spatial intelligence in MLLMs.
