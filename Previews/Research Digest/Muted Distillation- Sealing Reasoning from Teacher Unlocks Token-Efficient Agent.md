# Muted Distillation: Sealing Reasoning from Teacher Unlocks Token-Efficient Agents

**Source:** [ArXiv – cs.CL (Computation & Language / NLP)](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.28445)
**Imported:** 2026-11-24 15:22 UTC

## Abstract / Summary

arXiv:2611.28445v1 Announce Type: new Abstract: On-policy distillation (OPD) is commonly adopted as one of the components in post-training of large language models (LLMs), but in multi-turn agentic tasks we find that the student model increases their reasoning lengths after training. Our empirical analysis shows that the reasoning token lengths is affected by how much the student can distract the teacher's scores. We introduce Muted Distillation (MD), a simple variant of OPD that hides the student's reasoning from the teacher, so the teacher judges what the student does without the interference of student's reasonings. MD takes a few lines of code and adds no extra data or hyperparameters. On the knowledge-work benchmarks OfficeQA Pro and SpreadsheetBench, it reduces output tokens per episode by 57\% and 53\% compared with standard OPD, making the student more concise than it was before training. At the same time, it matches OPD's accuracy on OfficeQA Pro and improves pass@1 by 4.8 points on SpreadsheetBench.
