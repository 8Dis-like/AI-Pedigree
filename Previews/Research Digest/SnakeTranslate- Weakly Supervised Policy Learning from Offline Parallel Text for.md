# SnakeTranslate: Weakly Supervised Policy Learning from Offline Parallel Text for Simultaneous Translation

**Source:** [ArXiv – cs.CL (Computation & Language / NLP)](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.28055)
**Imported:** 2026-11-24 15:22 UTC

## Abstract / Summary

arXiv:2611.28055v1 Announce Type: new Abstract: Adaptive simultaneous translation requires reliable prefix translation and timely READ/WRITE decisions, but ordinary parallel corpora provide no action annotations. We present SnakeTranslate, which adapts loss-derived alignment to sampled causal histories for jointly learning both capabilities. The framework supports supervised fine-tuning (SFT) on parallel text and on-policy distillation (OPD) with reverse Kullback--Leibler (KL) divergence on student-generated translations. At inference, source and target tokens form one append-only interleaved history, enabling incremental decoding with cache reuse. On FLEURS, NTREX128, and WMT24++, our 4B model improves quality--latency trade-offs over streaming policies applied to the same offline-tuned backbone, with further gains from OPD. It also compares favorably with larger translation models at low and moderate latency, while higher-latency quality gaps remain on WMT24++.
