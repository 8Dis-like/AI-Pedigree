# Antom 3-in-1 Transformer: A Payment Foundation Model

**Source:** [HuggingFace – Daily Papers](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.25403)
**Imported:** 2026-11-25 06:00 UTC

## Abstract / Summary

Authors: Weiyu Han, Haoyu Jiang, Jie Sheng, Youxi Pan, Xianglong Liu, Zeqi Ren, Dongquan Yi, Yinhao Liu, Ruobing Zuo, Ze Zhao, Huilin He, Nairong Zhang, Xinyi He, Xiao Tan, Jun Wang, Pan Fang, Wei Zeng, Jianan Su, Kaixin Shen, Jingtong Wu, Hao Li, Shuai Chen, Jiangming Yang, Tianyi Zhang Upvotes: 18 Summary: We report the development of Antom 3-in-1 Transformer, a payment foundation model that integrates sequence, tabular, and graph representations for payment risk detection. The model contains approximately 7B parameters, with approximately 1.7B activated per task route. Antom 3-in-1 Transformer is pretrained to learn reusable behavioral and relational representations, primarily on Ant International's internal payment data. In a cross-business transfer experiment, pretraining improves the target business metric by 34.5% over the same architecture trained from scratch. With only 25% of the training labels, the pretrained model outperforms the full-label scratch baseline in Average Precision (AP) and Recall@0.5% on different merchant domains. In a four-month online experiment on Ant International's billion-scale payment platform, the model reduced the overall loss rate by 37.88% relative to XGB-Online.
