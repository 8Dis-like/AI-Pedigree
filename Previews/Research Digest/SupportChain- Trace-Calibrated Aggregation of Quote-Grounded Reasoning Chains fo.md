# SupportChain: Trace-Calibrated Aggregation of Quote-Grounded Reasoning Chains for Multi-Hop RAG with Small Readers

**Source:** [ArXiv – cs.CL (Computation & Language / NLP)](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.28519)
**Imported:** 2026-11-24 15:22 UTC

## Abstract / Summary

arXiv:2611.28519v1 Announce Type: new Abstract: Strong results in multi-hop retrieval-augmented question answering come from graph indexes read by models of 70B parameters or more, and building such an index calls a large language model over the whole corpus. We present SupportChain, which splits a question into hops, binds each hop's answer to a sentence quoted from a retrieved passage, estimates how reliable each execution is from its own trace, and merges several executions by the support of their answers, the pooled reliability of the executions that agree, stopping once support is high enough. With a 9B reader under the HippoRAG~2 protocol, SupportChain reaches 63.0 F1 on MuSiQue, above the best earlier result of 59.2, which used a 20B reader. With the same reader and embedder, it is significantly better than HippoRAG~2 on all five datasets, by up to 17.2 F1, and it builds no index. Merging by support is significantly better than majority voting, and than letting the reader choose among the candidates, on three of the five datasets and level with both on the other two. The reliability model needs only 100 answered training questions.
