# Typhoon OCR 2: From Thai Document Transcription to Multilingual, Multi-Task OCR

**Source:** [HuggingFace – Daily Papers](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.27494)
**Imported:** 2026-11-24 15:22 UTC

## Abstract / Summary

Authors: Surapon Nonesung, Natapong Nitarach, Sittipong Sripaisarnmongkol, Kunat Pipatanakul, Warit Sirichotedumrong Upvotes: 1 Summary: Typhoon OCR 1.5 focused on reading Thai documents. However, in real-world workflows, users often need to extract specific information or preserve the structure of an entire page for further processing. Typhoon OCR 2 handles both tasks in a single model, with prompt-driven extraction and structured full-page parsing. Full-page parsing covers Thai, English, and Chinese. We release compact 2B and 0.8B models adapted from open document parsers through supervised fine-tuning on curated Thai and public multilingual data, followed by Group Relative Policy Optimization (GRPO) with task rewards and a rule penalizing unfinished responses. Among evaluated open models, Typhoon OCR 2 2B scores highest on Thai MDPBench (80.7 versus 68.9 for Typhoon OCR 1.5) and on ThaiOCRBench (59.91 versus 40.45), which covers recognition, extraction, and document understanding. The 2B model remains close to its base on English olmOCR-bench (84.7 versus 84.4) and on OmniDocBench, which evaluates English and Chinese together (90.97 versus 91.03). On MDPBench, GRPO reduces the mean share of pages with repetition loops for the 2B model from 3.2% to 0.7%, with a smaller and statistically uncertain score gain for the 0.8B model. We release the model weights, Python package, and evaluation suite.
