# One Step, Four Clocks: How GUI Agents Fall Out of Step

**Source:** [ArXiv – cs.AI](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.31522)
**Imported:** 2026-11-25 06:00 UTC

## Abstract / Summary

arXiv:2611.31522v1 Announce Type: new Abstract: GUI agents can choose correct actions yet fail as interfaces change during deliberation. Latency alone cannot explain these failures because actions may become invalid at different interaction stages. We model four events: environment change, observation availability, decision commitment, and action receipt (EODA). We introduce ChronotopeGym to study these failures under Paused, Replay, and Live execution. Across five tasks, agents can fail in Live despite succeeding when responses remain valid, even when actions meet deadlines. On Moving Balloon, updating a pending click raises success from 0% to 100% without changing response time. Our accelerator-resident GUI controller, Chronotope, maintains compact interaction history and selectively keeps, updates, or redecides pending actions. Compared with Agent S3, Chronotope reduces median end-to-end latency by 85.5% for a 27B model, expanding its performance envelope to 100% Live success at an 8-second deadline versus 0%. Chronotope enables real-time GUI control through compact interaction history, knowing when to preserve, revise, or replace actions, and an overall efficient architectural design that obviates verbose reflection, reasoning, and grounding calls.
