# GHC:Expanding Residual Interfaces in Hyper-Connections

**Source:** [ArXiv – cs.LG (Machine Learning)](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.31350)
**Imported:** 2026-11-25 06:00 UTC

## Abstract / Summary

arXiv:2611.31350v1 Announce Type: new Abstract: Hyper-Connections expand residual capacity across parallel streams, yet restrict every branch to narrow read and write interfaces: each branch still reads a single stream mixture and writes scaled copies of one output vector to all streams. To lift this bottleneck, we propose Grouped Hyper-Connections (GHC). By partitioning each branch into parallel groups along attention heads or FFN channels, GHC supplies each group with a distinct stream mixture and aggregates their separate outputs into each stream with learned weights. Extensive experiments across dense and Mixture-of-Experts (MoE) models show that GHC consistently improves upon Identity Hyper-Connections (IHC) with negligible parameter and memory-access overhead, increasing training efficiency by 35% over Pre-Norm and 10% over IHC. Moreover, expanding residual interfaces overcomes the diminishing returns of adding streams, allowing wider residual states to yield further gains.
