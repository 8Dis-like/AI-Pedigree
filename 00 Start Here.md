# Modern AI Pedigree

An editable Obsidian research vault based on *The Infinity Machine* / *哈萨比斯：谷歌AI之脑*. Built 2026-10-23. English is the default; original Chinese names are retained as aliases.

**Start with [[Modern AI Pedigree.canvas|Modern AI Pedigree]].** The dedicated [[11 Richard Sutton Lineage.canvas|Richard Sutton branch]] is included alongside the [[10 Hinton Lineage.canvas|Geoffrey Hinton branch]].

## Open in Obsidian

1. Unzip the package.
2. In Obsidian, choose **Open folder as vault** and select **AI Pedigree Vault**.
3. Open **Modern AI Pedigree.canvas**. Pan and zoom, move cards, edit their text and add labelled arrows normally.
4. Click an entity’s name to open its note, source links and PDF-page citations.

The canvases use the standard [JSON Canvas 1.0 format](https://jsoncanvas.org/spec/1.0/). No community plugin or custom theme is required. The files and notes stay editable. If importing into an existing vault, copy the **contents** of this folder to the vault root together; canvas links use those vault-relative paths.

## What is included

- 822 entity notes, including 519 people; 781 entities have book page references.
- 161 primary-source checked relationships, 375 book-reported relationships, and 1 disputed relationships withheld from canvas views.
- 65 external source records and 82 entities with a checked profile or institutional evidence link.
- 7 editable canvases, a name register, an evidence review queue, and the supplied PDF for portable page links.

## Read the map correctly

**● Human** (blue), **◆ institution** (purple), **■ company** (amber), **▣ lab/team** (green), **◇ other context** (grey). Text labels and symbols repeat the distinctions so color is not the only cue.

**V** edges have checked primary evidence. **B** edges report the book and still need external checking. Disputed edges remain in notes but are excluded from canvas views. The legend is present on every map.

Adviser, co-supervisor, postdoctoral mentor, colleague, coauthor, employee, founder and investor are distinct relationships. Arrows read source → labelled relationship → target; for example, Hinton → PhD adviser → Sutskever. Group boxes are navigation aids, not legal ownership or current reporting lines.

Dates belong to claims. A source accessed today may describe a past role. This is a historical community map, not a complete present-day directory. Web extensions are marked separately from book mentions.

## Reliability and coverage

All 505 scanned PDF pages received local OCR. The inventory includes narrative, forewords, acknowledgments and endnotes, with uncertainties retained. OCR can miss names, and many peripheral profiles remain unchecked; the register is a broad first pass, not a certified exhaustive index. The main branches prioritize checked relationships. Each entity with no verified affiliation is retained without an invented organizational connection.

See [[03 Evidence and Open Questions]] for corrections, source disagreements and the next verification work. Source and relationship records are also in `Data/` for later expansion. Changing a canvas does not automatically update its entity notes or the evidence data; update the claim record when changing a factual relationship.
