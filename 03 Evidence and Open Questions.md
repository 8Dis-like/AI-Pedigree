# Evidence and open questions

## Evidence standard

V: a checked primary source directly supports the relationship. B: the supplied book reports it; verification remains open. D: a conflicting or suspect claim withheld from canvases. Verified identity does not automatically verify every role in a person’s note. Dates and source scope control interpretation.

## Corrections and limitations

- [[People/Chris Maddison|Chris Maddison]]: The book’s PDF p. 219 calls Maddison a Toronto doctoral student. His CV records a Toronto MSc with Hinton (2012–2014) and Oxford DPhil with Teh and Doucet (2016–2020). Do not draw a Hinton → Maddison PhD edge.
- [[People/Alex Krizhevsky|Alex Krizhevsky]]: A completed MSc is confirmed. Do not infer a completed PhD from descriptions such as graduate student or Hinton student.
- [[People/Ilya Sutskever|Ilya Sutskever]]: Hinton’s roster gives 2012 while Toronto’s alumni reporting gives PhD 2013. Supervision is verified; the graduation-year discrepancy is preserved.
- [[People/David Silver|David Silver]]: Sutton and Martin Müller jointly supervised his Alberta PhD. Historical DeepMind roles are dated; this is not an assertion of current employment.
- [[People/Arthur Guez|Arthur Guez]]: Both Peter Dayan and David Silver supervised his UCL PhD; the book’s Silver-only description is incomplete.
- [[People/Richard Sutton|Richard Sutton]]: Separate adviser/student edges from collaboration. Bowling and Pilarski are colleagues/coauthors here. Degree-specific supervision for Precup and Linke needs a checked thesis record before upgrading the general mentorship edge.
- [[Institutions/EMBL-EBI|EMBL-EBI]]: The book’s apparent Cambridge University affiliation is not accepted. EMBL-EBI belongs to EMBL and is located near Cambridge.
- [[People/Noam Brown|Noam Brown]]: The book’s PDF pp. 424 and 499 claim an earlier DeepMind / Google DeepMind affiliation. His first-person profile instead documents FAIR (Meta), OpenAI and a CMU PhD. The book’s DeepMind claim remains disputed and is excluded from all canvas views.
- [[Institutions/Future of Humanity Institute|Future of Humanity Institute]]: Oxford’s Future of Humanity Institute and the separate Future of Life Institute must not be merged. The book’s rendering is ambiguous in one passage.
- [[People/Yann LeCun|Yann LeCun]]: Hinton was a postdoctoral mentor, not his PhD adviser. Meta roles in this map are historical; the linked NYU 2026 biography reports AMI Labs.
- [[Institutions/Openmind Research Institute|Openmind Research Institute]]: Sutton’s Amii profile identifies this institute. It has not been merged with the similarly named OpenMind robotics company.
